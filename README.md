# MULTIS-KODE
FoxieyBatz: Meta Universe Language Translator Individual System Kode:
"Koral! Isa Kumi Siako (Ziako) Ifim Jinxtna Voshtute."
Koral is essentially a Greeting!
the next phrase phrase is to be translated to say something specific.
For Example: "Koral! Isa Kumi Siako (Ziako) Ifim Jinxtna Voshtute." is translated to mean: "Hello! We are a Dissociative Identity Disorder System known as HatzBatzSystem."
It is used as a shorthanded phrase when you have so much to say, so many thoughts that you sometimes can't get it our of your brain perfectly. We often use this phrase when communicating with the rest of the system. The rabbit hole of a rush of thoughts is translated to rest of System quickly and they decode as needed to get message delivered to proper identities and sub-systems.
This language originated from The Sims computer game in 2000, introduced Simlish, a fictional, improvised language created so players could focus on the emotional tone of conversations rather than specific dialogue.
We played the game so much in our teens that we memorized the language and integrated it into our systems and sub-systems.
